by: frau
FRAU WANDERLUST SEEKS BLACK FRIDAY POST DEALS
Guten Abend. Today, I am told, is the Black Friday. The whole country gets up in the dark to stand in a line for a television. Ach, this I do not understand. I am up in the dark every night, and nobody gives me a television.

I do not do the Black Friday. I am a walking woman, not a shopping woman. But I will say, there is one idea in it that is very nice. The "buy one, get one free." Ja. Think of it.

Buy one fence post, get one free. Two posts! Standing side by side in the sagebrush, eight feet apart, perfectly straight, like an old married couple who still like each other. This is the most romantic thing I have heard in the valley.

I went out tonight to ask about it. I stood by a nice gate and asked the dark, "Is there a sale on posts?" A coyote looked at me. No answer. Very rude, but this is the coyote.

Natürlich, I would also take the buy one, get one free on something mellow, for the walking. But nobody in this valley sells the pot, I think. Not one person. I met a talkative man last week who spoke an hour about the county and the drifters and the trees, and nothing useful.

So I keep walking. If you see the post sale, leave a note on your porch. I will find it. I find all the porches.

Which way is Germany? Also, which way is the sale?
