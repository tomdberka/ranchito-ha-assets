BULLETIN: HIVE COUNCIL DENIES GROUNDING AMERICA
BULLETIN. By order of the Hive Council, Mt. Blanca Installation. To be read in full. *Hssss.*

[photo: ev-20261112-hive-council-seal.jpg | The seal of the Hive Council, reissued for a second consecutive bulletin. The Council is busy this week.]

Today the FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop, citing problems with air traffic control communications. For roughly four hours, nothing human left the ground. Flight cancellations are expected to stretch into the weekend.

The Hive Council states for the record that the installation had nothing to do with this. The Council does not require air traffic control. The Council has never once asked permission.

Subcommandant Sazsk accepts the Council's statement. Privately, he has his own theory: Washington did it to itself. An agency that cannot see a lit craft over Mt. Blanca for eleven years running cannot be expected to hear its own radios. *Sssss.*

He notes one consequence. With no airliners crossing the valley, the sky above Mt. Blanca is unusually empty. Should a civilian wish to inspect it personally, from as close as the road allows, nothing will be in the way. Not that anyone should look.

The Nuggets host Houston on Nov. 20 in the Emirates NBA Cup. The team will travel by bus if it must. *Hsss.*

WHAT TO PACK FOR THE POST HUNT
Guten Abend! Many people ask me, "Frau, what do you bring when you are walking the fences at night?" Nobody has asked me this, but I am telling you anyway.

First: go at night, natürlich. In the daytime the sun up here is very strong, and a lady can get sunburned looking at posts. At night the posts are cool, the cows are sleeping, and nobody is shouting from the porch. Usually.

Second: pack light. Even the food. You cover more ground this way. Here is my own supper, which I photographed myself:

[photo: ev-20261113-midnight-feast.jpg | A sumptuous feast for the night search: two raisins, one sunflower seed, one pretzel, and a square of chocolate, already tasted.]

Two raisins, one sunflower seed, one pretzel, and a little square of chocolate with only one corner missing. On a napkin, ja, because I am not an animal. This is enough for three or four kilometers of fence, or maybe one, I lose count.

Third: bring your eyes. The post does not announce itself. You will know it.

Ach, and maybe you need more snacks than this if you find someone in the valley selling this legal pot I am hearing so much about. I have asked very politely at many gates. Nobody sells. A nice talkative man told me for one hour about the county. Nothing useful. Which way is Germany?
