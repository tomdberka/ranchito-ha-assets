INTERNET COLLAPSES; BOARD NOT FOOLED
The Board wishes it entered into the record that the internet broke this week, in what one national outlet calls the highest number of weekly outages in the past twelve months.

The Board has reviewed the official explanations:

- A large bank blamed an internal software bug that delayed transactions with other banks.
- An airline cited "a technology problem."
- The FAA said a flood of network traffic destabilized older equipment, days after the longest nationwide ground stop since 9/11.
- Microsoft cited a months-old bug, increased traffic and reduced server capacity.

Four different excuses. The Board asks: who benefits?

Experts say it is not hacking, just institutions rushing to patch old code, and the patching knocking things over. The Board finds this suspiciously reasonable.

The Board did observe a light on in the Ranchito container Monday evening, the same day the bank went down. The Board did not see what was plugged in. The Board did not need to. The container has a solar array, a wind turbine, and a clear line of sight to the mountain. That is a relay.

The Board observes a moment of silence for InfoWars. As Alex told the Board, more or less, the internet was always going to be shut off by "them" so you'd have to buy the shortwave radio. The Board bought two.

A new sign went up this afternoon beside the others: UNPERMITTED SERVER FARM. Costilla County is zoned.
