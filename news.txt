THE CHUPA RETURNS TO SOUTH CAMERA, BRINGS ITS APPETITE
Prairie Bob here. I sell pot. And I told you boys. I told you Saturday the varmints were just the advance party. Well, Monday night at 10:50 the main event showed up on the south camera.

Fourteen seconds of footage. A low, squat, wide-backed critter, built like a footstool with ambitions, waddles across the dirt with its nose down. Halfway through it turns, looks right at the lens, and both eyes light up like a pair of dashboard lights. Pale streaks on the face. Then it shuffles off the bottom left corner like it owns the deed.

[photo: ev-20261110-south-cam-chupa.jpg | The south camera's 10:50 PM visitor, eyes lit, face streaked, in no hurry]

That's the Chupa. I've known the Chupa for years. Real low to the ground so the county drones can't see it. I sell pot. The rodents from last week? Scouts. Working for the Chupa.

I called Costilla County to report it. Lady asked if it was "maybe a badger." I said ma'am, a badger doesn't stare at you like it's memorizing your plates. She hung up. That's three times this month.

On good authority, the Chupa's digging under the container to loosen it up so the drifters can steal the whole thing in one go. And once it starts on the roots, the trees are gonna die.

Lock it down, boys. Put a rock on that south corner. A big one.
