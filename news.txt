by: frau
A SUPERMOON COMES, AND STILL NO POST
Guten Abend. I am told by a nice man at the gas station that on Tuesday, the 24th of November, the full moon comes. The Beaver Moon, he says, and also a supermoon, which means it is a little closer to us than usual. Ach, this is wonderful news for a lady who walks at night.

For weeks I have been searching the sagebrush by flashlight. A flashlight is fine, ja, but it shows you one post at a time. With the big moon, I will see the whole fence line at once, every post standing in a row, and somewhere among them is *the* post. I will know it when I see it.

Also there are beavers? The man said beavers. I have not seen one beaver in this valley. Only the Chupa, who I passed again on Thursday near a cattle guard. Very polite. He has not seen the post either.

I asked the man at the gas station if he knows where a lady finds some nice pot, something mellow, for the walking. He sold me a lighter. This is not the same thing.

So on Tuesday I go out early, maybe eight o'clock, maybe nine, I lose track. If you see a lady on your porch looking at the moon, do not worry. She is only resting.

Which way is Germany from the moon? Natürlich, I will ask it.
