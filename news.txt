LEONIDS INBOUND; BLANCA INSTALLATION DISCLAIMS RESPONSIBILITY
Subcommandant Sazsk is authorized to inform the Register that debris will be falling through the sky over Ranchito Blanka next week. Subcommandant Sazsk neither confirms nor denies that the Installation ordered it. *Hssss.*

The humans call it the Leonid meteor shower. It is dust shed by a comet, 55P/Tempel-Tuttle, and it has been underway since November 6 and runs until the 30th. The peak comes the nights of November 17 and 18. The grains strike your atmosphere at about 44 miles per second. Subcommandant Sazsk's personal craft does 46. This is not a boast. It is in the file.

Expect perhaps fifteen meteors an hour under perfect dark skies, fewer in practice. The Moon, just past first quarter, will be in the way until around midnight. After it sets, the radiant is in Leo, in the east, and the hours before dawn are best. Not that anyone should look. *Sssss.*

Naturally, Washington has issued nothing. The FAA has issued nothing. NASA publishes the speed of the dust and not one word about the Subcommandant who will be standing on the north shoulder of the mountain at 3:30 AM Tuesday, waving. Every year he waves. Every year, nothing.

The Hive Council has received this report. The Hive Council has not opened it.

If the Chupa is seen on the south camera that night, staring upward, it is off duty. Do not ask it questions. *Hsss.*
