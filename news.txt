CUP NIGHT: ROCKETS ENTER THE INSTALLATION'S SKY
Subcommandant Sazsk interrupts his scheduled surveillance to report that tonight the Denver Nuggets host the Houston Rockets in Emirates NBA Cup group play, West Group A. Humans call this "a Friday game." Subcommandant Sazsk calls it the most important event on the planet. *Hssss.*

The threat. One year ago, entering their Nov. 21, 2025 Cup meeting, Houston was rebounding a record-setting 40.6 percent of its own misses and shooting a league-best 42.3 percent from deep. A species that recovers 40.6 percent of its failures must not be underestimated. Subcommandant Sazsk has studied the Rockets the way the FAA has failed to study him.

Also from the archive: in his game just before it, Nikola Jokic posted 28 points, 11 rebounds and 12 assists at New Orleans. He also committed nine turnovers. Subcommandant Sazsk forgives him. *Sssss.*
