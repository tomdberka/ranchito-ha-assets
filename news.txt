NIGHT CORRESPONDENT INTERVIEWS SOUTH CAMERA VISITOR
Guten Abend. Zis is Frau Wanderlust, reporting from somewhere near ze south camera. I vas not trespassing. I vas inspecting.

Last night I am valking ze sagebrush viz my little flashlight, looking for ze post. You know ze vun. Straight, proud, not too tall. And who do I meet? A small grey creature, long in ze body, pale in ze belly, vun eye glowing like a tiny lantern.

[photo: ev-20261108-south-camera-visitor.jpg | The south camera's guest of the evening, one eye lit]

Herr Prairie Bob says zis is a rodent. Ach, I do not know. It has ze legs of a fox and ze manners of a cat. I asked it politely, "Have you seen ze post?" It did not answer. Very rude. Or very shy. Zis is ze same thing in ze dark, ja?

Ve stood together a long time. It looked at me viz ze vun eye. I looked at it viz both. Zen it valked avay, very slow, like it vas ze owner of ze property. Natürlich, I understood. I also feel zis vay on ozzer people's land.

I counted ze fence posts on ze vay out. Seven. No, nine. Vich vay is Germany?

Ze post vas not here tonight. But ze creature is goot company. I vill come back.

— Frau Wanderlust, Location Unknown
